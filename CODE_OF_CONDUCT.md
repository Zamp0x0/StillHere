# Code of Conduct

StillHere follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful and harassment-free for everyone, whatever their background, identity or
level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use StillHere, including the people whose conversations it may process.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission. This includes chat logs, screenshots of chats, personal data and
  model outputs that reveal them.
- Using StillHere to impersonate a real person in order to deceive or harm someone.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private contact route). Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove content or block participants who break this code.
